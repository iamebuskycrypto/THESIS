# THESIS 0.6.1 release

The actual Mac acceptance report from September 23 showed working Apple/NVIDIA feeds, both Bitget market connections, the local Qwen model and a schema-valid paper decision on fictional inputs. These are integration results, not financial-reasoning validation.

None of four new free-form research assessments were accepted. Two failed a too-restrictive amount-citation gate; two were flagged by the same-model audit, sometimes for the wrong reason. That auditor also missed all four previously identified semantic mistakes. It cannot be treated as a reliable verifier.

This release changes research scope: Qwen selects an event category and source highlights; the application copies the complete eligible source passages exactly and preserves conditions and amounts. It presents saved market observations without inferring causal impact or how much news is priced in. It no longer generates a commercial-effect forecast, a conditional business claim or a research trading verdict. It does not repair Qwen's financial reasoning. Category and highlight usefulness still need review.

The one-call source brief replaces the three-call draft/audit workflow. Earlier reviews remain unchanged and readable with an explicit reliability notice. Research remains separate from the existing paper agent. Portfolio, journal, exports and monitoring remain available. The paper decision prompt, execution policy, agent.py and pipeline.py are unchanged.

The updater backs up code, refuses unrecognised edits, and does not edit observed run data. Its default check makes eight local model calls on fictional inputs and then opens THESIS. Source copying and brief completion cannot be compared with earlier free-form assessment acceptance as an accuracy improvement.
